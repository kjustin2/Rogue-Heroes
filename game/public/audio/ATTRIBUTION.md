# Audio attribution

Every file here is CC0 / public domain (no credit required); credit is given anyway.
Fetch and transcode with `scripts/audio-fetch.sh` + `scripts/audio-build.sh` (ffmpeg).

## Sound effects (`sfx/`)
- Gunshots (rifle, carbine, pistol, scattergun): "Gunshot Sounds" by Tabasco, https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
- Bangs, cannons, booms (bolt, cannon, blast, boom groups): "25 CC0 bang / firework SFX" by rubberduck, https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx
- Hits (metal, punch, soft, plate, wood): "Impact Sounds" by Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

## Real firearms (`sfx/ar15_*` ... `sfx/ppshburst_*`)
- "The Free Firearm Sound Library" by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney (CC0, no rights reserved),
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library. Single shots and bursts cut from the long recordings by
  `scripts/audio-slice.py`: AR-15, AK-47, SKS, Carl Gustav M45, PPSh, Tikka, Marlin 336, Model 1894, Mosin Nagant, Savage 10,
  Arisaka, 1911, Walther PPQ, Bersa, Ruger Mark III, S&W 642, Ruger Single Six, M1917, Mossberg, Model 12, Nova.
