# Fun ideas, round 2 (2026-10-07)

Owner: "Which ones are boring? Could they be replaced with something more fun? Are there ones we want to just remove so we have a
focused set of only fun things? Also the maps: are they all fun?" Every unit action, defense, support power, base upgrade, tech node,
map item, map hazard and map, reviewed. Pick by number (⭐ = recommended); only the picks get built, one tested round at a time.
Delete this file once the picks are implemented.

## A. Unit action cards (the generic ones every unit shares)
1. ⭐ **CUT Crouch.** A passive stance with an invisible accuracy bonus and a slower next move: nothing happens on screen. Replace it
   with **Dive**: a 4m sprint-roll that makes the trooper hard to hit for the rest of the turn (shots aimed at it scatter wide).
2. ⭐ **MERGE Strike + Push into one "Strike" that always throws.** Two near-identical melee cards (a rush and a hit, or a rush and a
   shove). Every melee hit now knocks the target back, so one card is both the damage and the throw.
3. ⭐ **CUT Artillery Deploy.** A whole turn of planting outriggers before it may fire, and packing up costs another: a ritual, not a
   choice. The gun simply can't fire on a turn it moves. Remove the card and the rule text.
4. **SIMPLIFY Runabout Load / Unload.** Two cards and a ferry ritual. Option a: click the car with a trooper selected and it hops in;
   the car's Unload card stays. Option b: cut carrying entirely and make the Runabout a jump buggy that launches off slopes.
5. ⭐ **AUTO-CREW posts (cut the Man / Leave cards).** Walking onto a free post crews it; moving off leaves it. The same play with two
   fewer cards.
6. **Mortar Smoke order → "Walking Fire".** Smoke is the same soft utility we cut from the decks. Swap it for three lighter shells
   walking down a line.
7. ⭐ **CUT Tank Hull Down.** An invisible 30% passive no one sees. Replace it with **Run Over**: a tank that drives through troopers
   flattens them (they are thrown aside and hurt), the Chop Bike's slash at tank scale.
8. ⭐ **CUT the Marksman's MARK** ("allies hit it easier this turn"). An invisible buff; the piercing shot is the fun part, and the Mark
   was the last thing the cut Optics/scan family hung off.
9. KEEP: Grenade, the trooper hop (Jump over walls), Heavy suppression, Flamer FEAR, and every unit's own verb (Punch, Hook, Reel,
   Boost, Burrow, Throw, Ride, Shove, Slam, Detonate). They are visible and physical.

## B. Defenses (the base's build deck)
10. ⭐ **CUT the four buildable manned posts** (Gun Post, Mortar Pit, Rocket Post, Flame Post) from the decks. Seven gun emplacements
    is too many: posts plus Gun Turret, Mortar Turret and MG Bunker. The posts stay on the maps as things to grab.
11. **CUT MG Bunker.** It is a Gun Turret with more armour. Bastion keeps the Mortar Turret as its own.
12. **CUT Blast Wall.** A passive wall that blocks shots. Sandbags stay as the one cheap cover piece.
13. ⭐ **ADD Jump Pad** (defense): your OWN launch pad. A trooper that ends a move on it is flung 12m forward. It reuses the map
    launch-pad code, and it pairs with the Spring Trap (theirs fly back, yours fly forward).
14. **ADD Harpoon Tower** (defense): a post that drags the nearest foe in range to its foot each turn, reusing the Hookshot's pull.
    Good next to a minefield or a flame post.

## C. Support powers
15. ⭐ **Sentry Drop (Bastion starter) → Boulder Roll.** A giant boulder rolls down a line, flattening and bowling over every trooper on
    it (the Skater's bowling at map scale). Heavies stop it.
16. ⭐ **CONSOLIDATE the "bombs on a spot" family.** Airstrike, Cluster, Barrage, Gun Run, Rail Strike and Napalm all read the same.
    Cut **Cluster** (Barrage covers it) and **Rail Strike** (Gun Run covers it).
17. KEEP: Shockwave, Tank Drop, Paradrop, Minefield Drop, Napalm, Airstrike, Barrage, Gun Run.

## D. Base upgrades
18. KEEP Fortress Cannon, Income, the second base order (each is a real economy choice).
19. ⭐ **ADD Catapult** (base upgrade): once a turn, the base flings one fresh trooper onto any spot in range. An instant forward deploy
    that arcs across the map.

## E. Tech tree
20. ⭐ **CUT all four specialization pairs.** They are invisible stat bumps (+25% damage / HP, accuracy, splash), the same family the
    owner cut from the decks:
    - Breaching Rounds / Bulwark Training,
    - Optics / Ghillie,
    - Thermobarics / Cluster Munitions,
    - Reactive Plating / Hunter Rounds.

    The tree becomes only doctrines that unlock units and items you can see.
21. ⭐ **FOLD Sharpshooters away.** It unlocks only the Rail Strike (cut in 16). Recon Doctrine keeps the Marksman and Flak Track.

## F. Items on the maps
22. ⭐ **THEME the field posts per map.** Every map carries the same five manned post kinds in mirrored pairs (ten posts), which is the
    main reason the maps feel alike and cluttered. Keep two kinds per map, chosen for its story:
    - Ironworks: cannon + flame;
    - Crossfire: gun + mortar;
    - Karak: mortar + rocket;
    - Dust Bowl: rocket + gun;
    - Causeway: cannon + gun;
    - Verdant: flame + mortar.
23. KEEP supply caches, red barrels, launch pads, thin ice and the freight train: all visible, all physical.

## G. Map hazards
24. ⭐ **Dust Bowl Sandstorm → Dust Devil.** The sandstorm is an invisible accuracy drop, and Dust Bowl is the slowest map (16 of 24 test
    games undecided). The Dust Devil is a visible twister that wanders the central lane each turn and flings anyone it touches.
25. ⭐ **Causeway Ion Storm → Icebreaker.** "Units limited to 1 AP" is frustrating and invisible. The Icebreaker is a ship that ploughs
    the channel every few turns, shattering the thin ice and the ice bridges and dunking whatever stands on them (warned a turn early,
    like the train).
26. **Verdant Lightning → Stampede.** Lightning every turn is random. A herd of cattle crosses a marked lane every few turns, bowling
    troopers over (warned a turn early).
27. **Karak Collapse → Rolling Boulder.** A huge boulder rolls down the ziggurat's stair lane on a timer (the Boulder Roll in 15 shares
    its code).
28. KEEP Ironworks Slag + Train, and Crossfire Barrage (add item 29).

## H. Make each map its own (one signature twist each, on top of G)
29. **Crossfire Basin: a minefield strip** across one flank (visible flags, the minefield code), so the flank costs nerve.
30. ⭐ **Dust Bowl: tighten it.** Pull the bases about 15% closer and add an oil geyser on each side that launches whoever stands on it
    when it blows. Target: the undecided games drop to the median map's level (about 9 of 24).
31. **Ironworks: a conveyor belt.** A strip of floor that carries anything standing on it 4m each turn (toward the slag, naturally).
32. **Verdant Pass: a river raft.** A raft drifts down the mill stream and back; troopers can ride it across.
33. **Ruins of Karak: a lever.** Pulling it (a trooper beside it, 1 AP) drops a column of the colonnade across the stair lane, opening or
    closing a route.
34. **Frozen Causeway: the ice cracks** under a vehicle that stops on it (already partly there), now with a visible spider-web stage
    each turn.
