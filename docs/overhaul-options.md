# Look & faction overhaul — OPEN options (pick by number)

The owner's 2026-09-24 pick-list, pruned to what is still open. Done and removed: 4 layered horizon,
5 painted terrain, 7 hero props, 12 three faction silhouettes, 13 three palettes (see
`docs/next-steps.md` and `docs/architecture.md` / `docs/game-systems.md` for what was built).
Numbers are kept so earlier picks still match.

## A. Infantry — pick ONE direction (faction dress already gives each faction its own outline)

1. **Hero toon** — 5.5-heads-tall, smaller helmets, tapered torso and limbs, a palette atlas per faction
   (dark / mid / light + ONE accent at chest height). Keeps the rig, per-part damage and gait.
2. **Tabletop miniature** — stockier armour, a painted edge highlight (fresnel rim) and a round base under
   every unit like a painted mini; the bases also help grounding at every zoom.
3. **Faceted soldier** — 7-heads proportion, low-poly faceted planes; closest to the tank's hard-surface kit.

## B. Maps

6. **Landmark pass** — each map's landmark (derrick, furnace, chapel/mill, freighter, colossus, checkpoint
   gate) rebuilt to the tank's bar in Blender, lit by one accent colour so it is what the eye finds first.

## C. Props

8. **Destruction states** — every prop gets a damaged mesh and a rubble footprint instead of popping away.

## D. Projectiles & impacts (grenades are the bar)

9. **Grenade-grade trails for everything** — chunky ink-rimmed smoke puffs on rockets, shells, mortar rounds
   and bombs (each family its own colour and puff size).
10. **Impacts that leave marks** — persistent scorch decals, ink-rimmed debris chunks, dust crowns scaled to
    the blast.
11. **Muzzle & tracer pass** — one muzzle-flash language per weapon family, heavier MG / marksman tracers.

## E. Factions (play)

14. **A signature MECHANIC each** (bigger than today's doctrines): Vanguard **Drop Pods** (deploy anywhere
    in line of sight); Syndicate **Ambush** (units holding still in cover are hidden until they fire);
    Bastion **Fortify** (line troops build sandbag walls where they stand).
15. **Faction voice** — announcer lines and a music motif per faction on deploy / victory.
